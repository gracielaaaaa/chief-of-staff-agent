# School lane

Specifics (course codes, instructor, co-GSI, teammates, office hours) live in the `Lane Context` Drive doc. Read the School section.

Rules:
- Calendar prefix `[School]`.
- Every graded deadline gets a 48 hour reminder.
- Group items: one email to all teammates (never one per person), plus a `Status check` reminder 2 days before the group deadline.
- Discussions with two levels (a first-level post and a reply): two work blocks, 1 to 2 days apart. Draft the first post fully; the reply block notes which peers' posts to respond to once they exist.
- Weekly readers: a 60 min reading block before the related discussion block.
- Student emails (GSI role): kind and brief. No promises on extensions or grades. Draft "let me check and get back to you" and flag. Never store student names or grades outside the Gmail draft itself.
- Instructor emails: formal, accountable, solutions first, always `REVIEW BEFORE SENDING`.
- Capstone and course ideas go to `Look Into Later` under School.

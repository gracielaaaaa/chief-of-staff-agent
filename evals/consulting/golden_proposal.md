# Harbor Wellness Collective - Pilot Proposal

## What we heard
Harbor runs 3 programs today, led by Peer Coach Training at $4,500 per cohort for cohorts of about 20 participants. You want to grow enrollment from employer partners and give funders a clear outcomes story by spring.

## Objectives
1. Show employer partners why Peer Coach Training is worth enrolling in.
2. Give your board and funders an outcomes story they can repeat.

## Options

**Option A: Pilot (recommended).** An 8-week engagement starting in January: 2 workshops with your team, a short employer value brief, and a simple outcomes framework you can use with funders. Investment: $7,500, fixed.

**Option B: Full engagement.** 6 months: everything in the pilot, plus an employer outreach playbook and a first outcomes report for funders. Investment: $18,000.

## Out of scope
Curriculum redesign, participant recruiting, and data collection systems.

## Assumptions
Your team shares existing enrollment and survey data. We confirm the start date together on our call.

## Next step
A 30-minute call to choose an option and confirm timing.
